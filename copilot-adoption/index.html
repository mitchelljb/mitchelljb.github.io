How I help

How I help

  1. Question the question.

    What should Copilot actually help with? Which tasks, which teams, which goals.

  2. Secure foundation.

    Insight into permissions and sharing, so Copilot only shows what people are allowed to see.

  3. Practical scenarios.

    Concrete examples per role, in the tools people already use: Teams, Outlook, Word, PowerPoint and Excel.

  4. Champions and guidance.

    A group of champions, short sessions and ongoing support, so Copilot becomes part of the daily routine.

  5. Measure and improve.

    Looking at real usage and adjusting where people get stuck.
